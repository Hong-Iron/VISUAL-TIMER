    How to play

    • Drag the dial (or the knob) to set time. It clicks on every tick. Hold Shift for fine steps.
    • Tap the knob to start or pause.
    • Scroll over the timer to add or take away a step.
    • Drag the body or the background to tilt it. Let go and it wobbles back.
    • Poke the body and it hops. Tap the green light to change colour.
    • Tap the range badge (60 MIN) to switch the dial between minutes and hours.
    • Every finished focus session drops a tomato on the desk. Poke them.
    • Mini window (the small screen button) keeps just the timer's face on top of your other windows.
    • Your sessions are saved in this browser. Open Record to see them, then Share my record for a brag card.
